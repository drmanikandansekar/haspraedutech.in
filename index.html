About Us

SCHOLARLY COMMUNICATION

Research Article Writing Support & Guidance

Build a clear, coherent and well-organised manuscript while keeping the researcher's own data, analysis, authorship and academic responsibility at the centre.

01

Research Idea & Article Structure

Clarify the study objective, research questions, contribution and logical structure of the manuscript.

02

Literature Review & Research Gap

Organise relevant studies, compare methods and findings, and explain how the present work relates to prior research.

03

Methodology & Results Presentation

Present experimental or computational methods transparently and improve the explanation of tables, figures, equations and metrics.

04

Academic Language & Formatting

Improve readability, grammar, flow, headings, citations, reference formatting and compliance with author instructions.

05

Journal Selection & Submission Preparation

Learn how to assess journal scope, indexing claims, publication ethics, article types and submission requirements.

06

Reviewer Response Guidance

Plan point-by-point responses, map revisions to reviewer comments and communicate changes respectfully and clearly.

Our guidance supports ethical research communication. Authors remain responsible for originality, data integrity, citations, authorship decisions and final submissions. Publication or acceptance is never guaranteed.

QUICK INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful information about our learning programmes and research guidance.

Are the workshops conducted online?

Delivery mode is announced for each event. Check the individual event details for the confirmed format.

Who can attend the programmes?

Depending on the topic, programmes may be suitable for UG/PG students, research scholars, faculty members, researchers and working professionals.

Can I get support for a research article?

Yes. Guidance may cover manuscript structure, literature review organisation, figures and tables, academic language, referencing, journal scope and reviewer-response preparation.

Are event dates and fees fixed?

Dates, fees, eligibility, certificates and registration instructions vary by programme and will be shared with each event announcement.
